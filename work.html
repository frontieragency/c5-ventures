Sonshine Glass

Glass & mirror · Orange County

Taken from invisible on Google to a steady stream of qualified local leads.

Problem
Invisible on Google — an old site with nothing indexed, so high-intent local searches went to whoever showed up first.
Approach
Deep research to find where they could win, then built the pages to own those niches — Eichler homes (a mid-century style known for floor-to-ceiling glass walls), commercial storefronts, multi-family retrofits, property managers, a page per service-area city — each feeding a lead-capture form that routes jobs to the shop.
Result
From zero to 20 indexed pages and a ~500x increase in daily search visibility in under two months since the relaunch — plus more than $300K in inbound lead value, including a $93K job now signed, materials ordered, from an Eichler homeowner who landed on the exact page built for them.
