## Post 05 — AI visibility

> First line is what shows before "more". Everything after it has to earn the tap.

Three weeks ago, ChatGPT had never heard of them.

A glass shop here in Orange County. Ask ChatGPT who to call for a frameless
shower enclosure and their name didn't come up. Not buried, not second page —
absent. Three of their competitors came up by name.

Now they come up first. Three different searches, three different parts of the
business.

Here's what most people miss: these assistants don't rank pages the way Google
does. They pull from reviews, directories, your own site, sometimes a Reddit
thread nobody remembers writing. If those sources don't clearly say what you do
and where you do it, you're invisible — however good your website looks.

So we track what ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI and Perplexity actually say about a
business, find where the gap is, and build the fix. Not a report with a to-do
list attached. The fix.

If you run a trade business in OC, go ask ChatGPT who it recommends for your
service. Right now, it takes ten seconds. It's usually the most uncomfortable
ten seconds of the week.

## Post 06 — Custom CRM

Pricing one job used to take five separate emails.

Same job, five suppliers, five times typing out the same sizes and the same
glass spec. Then waiting to see who came back cheapest — and hoping the quotes
were describing the same thing. Because if one shop reads "tempered" and another
reads "laminated," you're not comparing prices. You're comparing guesses.

Now it's one click. Pick the suppliers, the request goes to all of them with
identical wording, and the numbers that come back actually compare.

The rest of it: every job tracked from first call to paid invoice, proposals and
invoices generated from the same screen as the job, and a dashboard showing
pipeline value and win rate without anyone building a spreadsheet on a Sunday.

Built once, around how that shop already works — not a platform they had to
bend themselves to fit. And they own it. No monthly software fee, no per-seat
pricing, no "that's on our roadmap for Q3."
